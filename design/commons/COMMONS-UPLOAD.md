# Uploading the Blue Book chart to Wikimedia Commons

File: `Project_Blue_Book_UFO_sightings_by_year_1947-1969.svg` (made by `blue_book_chart.py`).
Commons had no chart of these figures as of 2026-10-24.

1. Sign in (or create a free account) at https://commons.wikimedia.org and open Special:UploadWizard.
2. Upload the SVG. Choose "This file is my own work" and the licence **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
3. Paste this as the description (the wizard also accepts it in "Source" if you pick "Provide wikitext"):

```
=={{int:filedesc}}==
{{Information
|description={{en|1=Bar chart of UFO sightings reported to the United States Air Force each year from 1947 to 1969 (Projects Sign, Grudge and Blue Book), split into explained and unidentified. 12,618 sightings in total, 701 unidentified.}}
|date=2026-10-24
|source={{own}}. Data: US Air Force, "UFO Fact Sheet", table "Total UFO sightings, 1947 - 1969" ([[:File:UFO fact sheet.pdf]]), a public domain US government work.
|author=Mike Johnston ([https://encounteratlas.com/ufo/blue-book/ Encounter Atlas])
}}
=={{int:license-header}}==
{{self|cc-by-sa-4.0}}
[[Category:Project Blue Book]]
[[Category:UFOs]]
[[Category:Bar charts]]
```

4. Do NOT add the chart to Wikipedia articles yourself (conflict of interest). Editors browsing
   Category:Project Blue Book find it; optionally leave one note on Talk:Project Blue Book saying the chart exists
   and that you made it.
